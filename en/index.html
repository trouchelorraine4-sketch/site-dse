Our Story

DSE Engineering was born from experience, a passion for engineering, and the purpose of transforming industrial challenges into opportunities for growth.

After more than two decades working in major industries, leading projects, developing processes and implementing improvements, its founder, Eng. Douglas Auler, identified the need to provide engineering solutions that are more accessible, closer to clients and aligned with the reality of small and medium-sized companies.

During six years of studies and professional experience abroad, this vision was strengthened through contact with different management methods, technologies and business cultures.

In 2022, DSE Engineering was founded. Since then, the company has been developing mechanical projects, process engineering, industrial consulting, continuous improvement, maintenance management, automation and engineering solutions.

Our philosophy is clear: identify and solve the root cause of problems, not just their effects.
